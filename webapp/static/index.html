Football Analytics Calculators

Interactive front end for the regression & GLM models from Football Analytics with Python and R (Ch. 3-6), fit on real play-by-play data via nfl_data_py.

Chapter 3 — Rushing Yards Over Expected (simple)

Model: rushing_yards ~ 1 + ydstogo. Enter yards to go and get the expected rushing yards on that play.

Chapter 4 — Rushing Yards Over Expected (multiple regression)

Model: rushing_yards ~ down + ydstogo + down:ydstogo + yardline_100 + run_location + score_differential. Optionally add the actual yards gained to compute RYOE for that play.

Chapter 5 — Completion Probability (simple)

Model: complete_pass ~ air_yards (logistic/Binomial GLM).

Chapter 5 — Completion Probability (multiple, with CPOE)

Model: complete_pass ~ down * ydstogo + yardline_100 + air_yards + pass_location + qb_hit. Optionally mark whether the pass was actually completed to compute CPOE.

Chapter 6 — Passing Touchdown Probabilities

Model: pass_td_y ~ pass_td_rate + total_line (Poisson GLM), reproducing the book's Mahomes/Super Bowl LVII example. "TD rate" is the QB's trailing average TD passes per game.

Fitted model details

Coefficients and sample sizes for each model, fit once at server startup on 2016–2022 play-by-play data.
